# Tools I Use to Simplify My Tasks

Here are a few tools I rely on to make my workflow faster and easier:

1. **JustPaste.it:** (https://justpaste.it/) Helps you write anything your mind/brain wants to dump. You can create an account if you wish. Most of my articles are written here—you can check my articles through my GitHub repo (https://github.com/itiyashah/A-Smart-Book-Course-Tracker), or directly on the website (https://justpaste.it/u/tiyashah) if you prefer not to visit the repo.
2. **Streamlit Community Cloud:** (https://share.streamlit.io/) Whenever I create a Streamlit/data app, I deploy it here (and I have made one—check it out!)-> (https://github.com/itiyashah/streamlit-quiz)
3. **UptimeRobot:** (https://uptimerobot.com/) Since my Streamlit tier is free, servers go to sleep after long periods of inactivity to save resources. I use keep-alive services to check my app every 5 minutes so it stays continuously active.
